# Sources To Browse

Where to hunt. Ordered by **hit-rate for this project**, not by fame. Everything saved from here
goes into the inbox — see [[Capture Workflow]].

---

## A. Start here — the two highest-signal galleries

* **Land-book** — `land-book.com`
  Filter by industry (*Furniture*, *Architecture*, *Real Estate*) and by style and colour. The best
  filters on the internet for exactly this kind of hunt. Start with Furniture.
* **Godly** — `godly.website`
  Hand-picked and genuinely well-judged. Small enough to browse end to end in one sitting.

## B. The rest of the gallery tier

| Site | Good for |
|---|---|
| `siteinspire.com` | Filter Architecture / Furniture, style Minimal |
| `minimal.gallery` | Restraint, whitespace, quiet type |
| `httpster.net` | Rougher, more current, less polished |

| `onepagelove.com` | Single-page structures |
| `curated.design` | Small, high taste |

| `thefwa.com` | Heavier and more technical; harvest ideas, not execution |
| `refero.design` | Searchable by page element — good when hunting one specific pattern |
| **`mobbin.com`** | **Mobile patterns. We're mobile-first and mobile is our weakest surface** |

## C. The actual competitive set

### Modular kitchens

bulthaup · Boffi · Poliform · Molteni&C (Dada) · Valcucine · Arclinea · SieMatic · Leicht ·
**Reform** (`reformcph.com`) · **Plum Living** · Naked Kitchens · Pluck · Nordiska Kök ·
Garde Hvalsøe · Modulnova · Cesar · Rational · next125

> Reform and Plum Living are the closest to our actual business — modern, made-to-measure, sold on
> configuration rather than heritage. Study those two hardest.

### Systems and storage — closest to the wardrobes story

**Vitsœ** (`vitsoe.com`) · String Furniture · USM · Rimadesio · Lema

> Vitsœ is arguably the best modular-system site on the internet, and its entire thesis is
> *planned around your life* — the nearest existing execution of **"Designed around YOUR home"**.

### Hardware — for the Hardware section

**Buster + Punch** · Blum · Hettich · Häfele · Corston · Joseph Giles

> Buster + Punch is the proof that premium hardware doesn't have to be presented as a parts
> catalogue. Directly relevant, because the real Google reviews describe us as a hardware store.

### Indian peers — study as a positioning check, mostly anti-reference

Livspace · Design Cafe · HomeLane · Sleek · Godrej Interio · Arrivae ·
Beautiful Homes (Asian Paints) · Nolte India

## D. Editorial and interior media — art direction, photography, layout

**The Modern House** (`themodernhouse.com`) and **The Local Project** are the two closest to our
thesis: a home presented as somebody's, not as a product shot.

Then: Dezeen · Yellowtrace · Est Living · Ignant · Sight Unseen · Cereal · Kinfolk · Openhouse ·
Nordic Design · AD India · Elle Decor India

## E. Typography

* `typewolf.com` — site of the day, **with the fonts named**. The fastest way to find a display face
* `fontsinuse.com` — browsable by industry
* Foundry sites are reference-grade in themselves: **Klim**, Grilli Type, Commercial Type, Dinamo,
  Pangram Pangram

> Relevant right now because **Fraunces and Inter have been released** — the display face is an
> open question, not a constraint.

## F. Motion and micro-interaction

`tympanus.net/codrops` (demos ship with source) · `designspells.com` (delight details) ·
agency sites — Locomotive, Immersive Garden, Cuberto

> Heavy motion is on our banned list. Harvest the **restraint** here, not the fireworks.

## G. Where to keep the wide net before it becomes a screenshot

* **Cosmos** (`cosmos.so`) — the strongest visual-saving tool right now, and unusually good for
  interiors
* `savee.it` · `are.na` (research-flavoured channels) · Pinterest (highest volume, lowest signal)
