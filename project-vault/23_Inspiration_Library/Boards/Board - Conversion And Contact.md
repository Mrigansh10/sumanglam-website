# Board - Conversion And Contact

The site converts to three things only: a showroom visit, a WhatsApp conversation, and a
consultation booking. No cart, no checkout, no accounts.

**Look for:** how a considered-purchase business asks for contact without a hard sell · booking
and enquiry forms worth imitating · where the ask sits in the page rather than shouting from a
sticky bar · how a physical showroom is made to feel worth travelling to · proof and credentials
presented without a testimonial carousel.

Note: V1 puts a "Leave a review" form on the homepage, asking for reviews from people who haven't
bought anything. That block is a candidate for deletion, not redesign.
