# Board - Avoid

Instructive failures. Saving something that breaks a rule is useful — it turns an abstract ban into
visible evidence.

## The standing bans

From [[Forbidden Patterns]] and [[17_Forbidden_Things]]:

* Glassmorphism, neon, dark-tech themes, aggressive popups, heavy motion
* Fake urgency, invented metrics, invented company history
* Anything that makes Sumanglam read as a hardware store, an ecommerce marketplace, a discount
  retailer, a tech startup, or a design agency

From the craft floor now binding on the redo:

* **Eyebrow/kicker labels above headings** — V1 uses them on nearly every section
* **Section numbers** (01 / 02 / 03) — V1's "why us" block uses them
* **Grids of same-size icon + heading + text cards used as page structure** — V1's core problem
* Traditional-Indian rendering of a modern modular product
