# Board - Imagery And Colour

The palette survives V2 — ivory `#faf8f5`, charcoal `#221d17`, bronze `#9a7945`, sand `#e5dfd4`.
Nothing else does.

Our real ceiling is imagery: about nine published inspirations, low-resolution, visually similar
3D renders restored through Cloudinary. So how a site **stages a small number of images** matters
more to us than how it handles a large library.

**Look for:** crops and scale changes · how one photograph is made to carry a section · colour used
as structure rather than decoration · where flat colour replaces an image entirely · treatment of
mixed-quality photography.
