# Board - Hero Treatments

The first screen: how a site makes its opening claim. Our current hero is a full-bleed render with
a centred headline over a scrim, and the floating header pill overlaps the headline at scroll-top.

**Look for:** what carries the claim — image, type, or emptiness · where the headline sits relative
to the image · how the nav coexists with it · whether the hero scrolls, holds, or splits.
