# Board - Motion And Interaction

The V1 Framer Motion system was "complete and frozen" — it is now **released**, so motion is open
again. Heavy motion remains banned, and Three.js, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, and Lenis are approved but
unused.

**Look for:** motion that does a job — revealing structure, marking arrival, holding attention on a
single image · restraint that still feels alive · what happens on scroll rather than on load ·
hover states worth having on desktop when mobile is the priority.
