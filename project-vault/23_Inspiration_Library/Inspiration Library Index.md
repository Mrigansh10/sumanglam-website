## How it works

Every capture becomes one note in `Refs/`, numbered in arrival order, with its image in
`attachments/`. Each note carries tags from **five facets at once** ([[Tag Vocabulary]]), so a
single screenshot appears on every board it belongs to — nothing gets filed in one place and lost.

Notes come in two tiers. **Light** is the default: the image, the source, the tags, one line on
what it's doing. **Deep** is written on request and adds the mechanism, why it works, how it
translates to our pages, and what breaks if it's copied.
